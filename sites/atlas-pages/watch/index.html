Real three-cube attempts, replayed

These are recorded test episodes of a BC policy trained only on two-cube demonstrations (phase-based controller, reserved test scenes, 23 Oct 2026; this model built 62 of 100 towers). Every frame is reconstructed from the simulator log: cube and gripper positions at 20 steps per second.

Reconstruction from logged positions: cubes are drawn axis-aligned because orientations were not logged, and the arm is reduced to the gripper. The success rate was measured under the metric in use at the time; a fingertip-contact check added later was not part of it (see journal, 24 Oct). Idle tails of failed episodes are trimmed.
